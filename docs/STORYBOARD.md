# Proposed animation storyboard
0–1s: closed envelope, seal touch/keyboard triggers intro; subtle light, no forced interaction delay.
1–2.5s: seal releases, flap rotates around fold; card remains inside until flap clears.
2.5–4s: card rises, camera approaches, transition to curtain scene.
4–6s: gold curtains part, reveal palace/fountain with gentle motion; hand off to native vertical scroll. Timing is proposed, adjustable after review.
Scroll: names → date/time → timeline → location with Dushkaj image and map link → closing. Optional couple image placement requires approval. Use subtle fades and parallax; no scroll hijacking. Skip and reduced motion lead directly to content.
